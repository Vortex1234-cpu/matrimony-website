Rajapalayam Matrimony

Child Safety Standards

Our published policy and practices for preventing child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE) on our platform.

Doc. Ref. CSP-2026-01 Effective 17 June 2026 Version 1.0

This document sets out the standards Rajapalayam Matrimony follows to keep minors off our platform and to prevent, detect, and respond to child sexual abuse and exploitation (CSAE). It applies to every profile, message, and piece of content on the app, and to our staff and moderators.

01

Who may use this platform

Rajapalayam Matrimony is a matrimonial service intended exclusively for adults seeking marriage. Every user must be at least 18 years old. Registration requires a valid government-issued photo ID, and the date of birth on file is checked before a profile is allowed to go live.

Any account found to misrepresent its user's age, or any profile suspected of belonging to a minor, is suspended immediately on discovery.

02

Zero tolerance for CSAE

We have zero tolerance for child sexual abuse material (CSAM), the sexualisation of minors, grooming, or any attempt to use our platform to contact, exploit, or endanger a child. This applies to profile photos, biographies, private messages, and any other content shared through the app.

Violating content is removed and the associated account permanently banned as soon as it is identified, and the matter is referred to the appropriate authorities described in Clause 05.

03

Safeguards on the platform

  • Every profile and photo is checked before it becomes visible to other users.
  • A report and block option is available on every profile and conversation.
  • Messaging is limited to users who have completed identity verification.
  • Accounts reported for suspected CSAE are reviewed and, where warranted, suspended pending investigation.
04

Cooperation with the authorities

We preserve information relevant to a CSAE report in line with applicable law and cooperate with the police, the Cyber Crime Cell, and other authorities investigating offences under the Protection of Children from Sexual Offences (POCSO) Act, 2012, and the Information Technology Act, 2000, including Sections 67, 67A, and 67B.

05

Report a concern

If you see a profile, message, or piece of content that you believe involves a minor, or that appears to be CSAM, please report it right away. Reports are treated seriously regardless of whether you choose to identify yourself.

In the app
Use the Report option on the profile or conversation in question.
Email

06

Review of this policy

This Child Safety Standards document is reviewed at least once a year, and sooner if our practices, the law, or platform policy requirements change. The version in force is always the one published at this address.
